# Sales Performance Dashboard & Analysis

**Problem Solved:** A retail company needed to understand their sales patterns, identify top-performing products, and optimize inventory management to increase profitability.

## Key Business Insights Delivered

- **Peak Sales Periods:** Identified optimal timing for marketing campaigns and inventory stocking
- **Product Performance:** Ranked products by profitability to guide purchasing decisions  
- **Revenue Trends:** Discovered seasonal patterns that informed budget planning
- **Customer Behavior:** Analyzed purchase patterns to improve product placement

## Business Impact

✅ **Cost Savings:** Prevented overstock of low-performing products  
✅ **Revenue Growth:** Highlighted high-margin products for promotional focus  
✅ **Strategic Planning:** Provided data-driven insights for quarterly business decisions  

## Analysis Highlights

The dashboard reveals critical business metrics through interactive visualizations:

- Monthly revenue trends with year-over-year comparisons
- Product performance rankings by units sold and profit margins
- Customer purchase frequency analysis
- Geographic sales distribution patterns

## Technical Approach

- **Data Processing:** Cleaned and standardized sales records from multiple sources
- **Visualization:** Created interactive charts using Python (Matplotlib/Seaborn)
- **Statistical Analysis:** Applied descriptive statistics to identify trends and outliers
- **Reporting:** Generated executive summary with actionable recommendations

## Files Structure

- `sales_analysis.ipynb` - Complete analysis with visualizations
- `data/` - Sample datasets (anonymized)
- `charts/` - Generated visualization outputs
- `requirements.txt` - Python dependencies
